Our Heritage

Established to serve the growing community of Maharagama, Central College has evolved into a premier Type 1AB institution within the educational landscape of the Western Province. Guided by our motto, we have consistently nurtured students to excel academically and ethically.

Over the decades, the college has continuously upgraded its infrastructure, introducing modern science laboratories, advanced IT centers, and extensive sports facilities to support both academic rigor and extracurricular excellence for thousands of enrolled students.
