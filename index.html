Welcome to Our College

Central College Maharagama stands as the singular Type 1AB Government School within the Maharagama Divisional Secretariat and electorate, situated across a picturesque 4.5 acre campus along Gammana Road. Established upon historical grounds dating back to World War II and officially chartered as a Central College in 1986, the college is home to over 1,100 students and an active faculty of 74 dedicated educators.

With our distinguished motto, "කළයුත්ත දැන එය කරනු" (Know what to do and do it), we provide complete secondary and advanced level education across Technology, Science, Commerce, Arts, and the 13-Year Guaranteed Education program. Our students consistently excel in all-island aesthetic competitions, national sports championships, and state examinations.

About Our College

History, governance, student leadership, and administration.

Historical Background & Heritage

Before 1986, this educational institute was officially known as the Practical School affiliated with the Maharagama Teachers' Training College (මහරගම ගුරු විද්‍යාලයට අනුබද්ධිත ප්‍රායෝගික විද්‍යාලය). Recognizing its vital regional significance and demographic growth, the Ministry of Education (Letter No: 3/G/G/S/F/1/3/7 dated 1986.08.25) formally named and declared the institution as WP/Jay/Maharagama Central College.

Today, situated adjacent to the National Institute of Education (NIE) and the National College of Education (NCOE), Maharagama Central College proudly stands as the only Central College serving the Maharagama electorate.
